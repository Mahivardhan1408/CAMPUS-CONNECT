How to Get an Item

1. Choose the item you want.

2. Click Contact Seller and provide your contact details in the email draft.

3. Agree on the final price, condition, and availability.

4. Arrange collection in a public campus location. Inspect the item before paying.

Important: Listings are examples. Contact details must be supplied by the actual seller.
